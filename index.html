About

I'm a PhD candidate in Linguistics at UC Santa Barbara, working on the acoustics and articulation of prosody — how speakers use pitch, timing, and articulatory movement to mark focus, and how those patterns transfer (or don't) when Taiwan Mandarin speakers produce English. My work combines acoustic analysis, electromagnetic articulography, and cross-linguistic comparison, with recent extensions into Estonian foot structure and Tokyo Japanese mora timing.

Before UCSB, I completed an MA in Linguistics at National Taiwan University and a BA in English at National Kaohsiung Normal University, where I trained and worked as an EFL teacher — a thread that still runs through my interest in second-language prosody.
